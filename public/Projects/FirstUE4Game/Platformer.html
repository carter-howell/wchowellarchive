An early Unreal Engine platformer where the player climbs upward while lava rises from below.

Build Summary

This was one of my first Unreal Engine projects. I built a vertical platformer in UE4 where the player has to climb faster than the rising lava. The project helped me learn level layout, gameplay triggers, iteration, and publishing a playable build to itch.io.
