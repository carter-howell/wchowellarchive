College Choice Hub

Software Project | 2022 | Completed

FirebasePHPSQLAJAX

A web app for students to share and track where friends planned to attend college.

Purpose

I built College Choice Hub as a student-facing site where people could look up where classmates planned to attend college. The original version used PHP, SQL, and AJAX, which made it one of my first larger web projects.

Migration

After the original hosting provider shut down the inactive site, I rebuilt and migrated the project to Firebase hosting and backend services so it could remain available as part of this portfolio.

  • Originally built with PHP, SQL, and AJAX.
  • Later migrated to Firebase hosting and backend services.
  • Designed for quick lookup without requiring a full account workflow.
